## v0.24.1

### English

#### Highlights

- Updated the default request limits for modern long-context and long-reasoning model workloads.

#### What Changed

- Increased the upstream response-header timeout and streamed-response idle timeout defaults to 5 minutes.
- Increased the default buffered request-body limit to 64 MiB.
- Positive timeout values below 5 minutes are raised to the 5-minute minimum; `0` remains an explicit opt-out for either timeout.
- Request-body limits below 64 MiB are raised to the 64 MiB minimum, while larger values are preserved.

---

### 中文

#### 亮点

- 面向现代长上下文、长推理模型请求，更新默认运行限制。

#### 变更内容

- 上游响应头超时和流式响应空闲超时的默认值均提升至 5 分钟。
- 默认缓冲请求体上限提升至 64 MiB。
- 超时设置为正数且低于 5 分钟时会自动提升至 5 分钟；`0` 仍可显式关闭对应超时。
- 请求体上限低于 64 MiB 时会自动提升至 64 MiB，更高的值保持不变。
