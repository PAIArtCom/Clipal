# Clipal mark: one local connection point

The Clipal mark joins two complementary white strokes into a compact, recognizable monogram. Its geometry remains unchanged: a circular opening on the left, a vertical spine and returning curve on the right, set against black.

The mark represents a local point of connection for coding tools and their chosen providers. It should suggest clarity, continuity, and control. It does not symbolize a measured latency, guaranteed availability, encrypted storage, arbitrary protocol conversion, or any other unverified product capability.

## Use in the landing page

- Keep the original geometry and clear black-and-white contrast.
- Use the compact mark beside the Clipal wordmark in navigation and the footer.
- In the hero routing illustration, place it at the center of a restrained physical surface. Incoming tool connections and outgoing native-protocol paths explain its role.
- Use mint for the selected route and primary action, not as a replacement for the mark’s white strokes.
- Label routing illustrations as examples. Do not dress the mark with fictional “live” status, random metrics, or decorative security promises.
- Keep depth subtle: a fine edge, a quiet highlight, and a grounded shadow. Avoid glowing orbs, excessive perspective, and repeated ornamental panels.

The surrounding design uses large typography, cold charcoal, generous spacing, and real console screenshots. The mark anchors that system; it should remain legible at favicon size and calm at larger scales.

## 中文说明

Clipal 标志由两段互补的白色曲线构成，保留现有几何与黑白对比。它表达编程工具与所选提供商之间的本地连接点，传达清晰、连续与掌控感，不代表未经验证的性能、安全或协议转换能力。

导航和页脚使用紧凑标志；首屏把它置于路由示意中心，以细边、轻微高光和阴影形成克制的实物感。薄荷绿只突出选中路线与主要操作。路由图必须明确标注为示意，不添加虚假的实时状态或指标。
