# Clipal mark: one local connection point

The Clipal mark joins two complementary white strokes into a compact, recognizable monogram. Its geometry remains unchanged: a circular opening on the left, a vertical spine and returning curve on the right, set against black.

The mark represents a local point of connection between coding tools and their chosen upstream providers. It communicates clarity, continuity, and local control. It does not symbolize a measured latency, guaranteed availability, universal credential encryption, arbitrary protocol conversion, or any other unverified capability.

## Use in the landing page

- Keep the original geometry and crisp black-and-white contrast.
- Pair the compact mark beside the `Clipal.` wordmark in navigation, social cards, and brand lockups.
- In the hero routing illustration, place it at the center of the local gateway surface (`127.0.0.1:3333` / `/clipal/*`), connecting incoming coding CLIs to outgoing native-protocol routes and compatible providers.
- Use the acid-green signal (`#c8ff3d`) for active routes and primary actions, never as a replacement for the mark’s white strokes.
- Label routing diagrams as illustrative examples; do not attach fabricated live status or unverified security/performance metrics to the mark.

The surrounding Signal Brutalism system uses oversized typography, near-black (`#0a0a0a`), warm paper (`#f1f0ea`), generous spacing, and real console screenshots. The mark anchors that system cleanly from favicon size to display scale.

## 中文说明

Clipal 标志由两段互补的白色曲线构成，保留既有几何比例与黑白对比。它表达编程工具与所选上游提供商之间的本地连接点，传递清晰、连续与本机掌控感，不代表未经验证的性能指标、全量加密或任意协议互转承诺。

在落地页与分享卡片中，紧凑标志与 `Clipal.` 字标配合使用；首屏将其置于本地路由示意的中心节点，连接上方编程客户端与下方原生协议及上游提供商。信号绿（`#c8ff3d`）仅用于突出当前激活路线与核心操作按钮，不替代标志本身的白色笔画；路由演示明确标注为示意数据，不附加虚构的实时状态。
